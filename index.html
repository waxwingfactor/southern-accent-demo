How it's done, and what I'd teach you

  1. Transcribe the videoElevenLabs Speech to Text gives the exact words with timings.
  2. Clone your voiceInstant Voice Clone from about a minute of your normal talking. You do this once and reuse it for every video.
  3. Remix the clone into a Southern accentElevenLabs Voice Remix. You describe the accent you want, from a light Texas lilt to a full drawl, listen to the options and keep the one that still sounds like you.
  4. Re-voice the scriptGenerate the same words in the Southern voice. Re-generate any sentence that's off. Names are the usual culprit.
  5. Lip-sync the videoUpload the original video and the new audio to sync.so (lipsync-2-pro). The mouth is redrawn to match the new speech.
